### Business Scenario

POWER BI DASHBOARD TASKS


## Dashboard 1 — Executive Overview

Management should be able to understand the company's overall performance at a glance.

Show:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Purchasing Customers
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Category
- Orders by Status
- Revenue Growth / Decline

**Goal:** Is the company performing well, and how is its performance changing?
