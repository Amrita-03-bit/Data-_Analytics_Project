# Company Analytics Project

## Project Objective

Analyze company data to identify important business insights related to employees, salaries, departments, clients, projects and tasks. The final analysis should present numerical findings as well as visual insights that can help a company understand its performance and make better decisions.

---

# Project Questions

## Employee & Department Analysis

### Q1
Analyze the number of employees working in each department and present the findings visually.

### Q2
Analyze the average salary of employees in each department and present the findings visually.

### Q3
Identify the department with the highest total salary and present the comparison visually.

### Q4
Analyze employee distribution across different cities and present the findings visually.

### Q5
Analyze employee performance across departments and present the findings visually.

---

## Salary Analysis

### Q6

### Q8
